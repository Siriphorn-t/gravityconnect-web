PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Gravity Connect Co. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Gravity mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, and gender (optional) when you create an account
  • Location data: approximate and precise location to enable nearby discovery features
  • User-generated content: GV Cards, messages, and other content you create
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and app interaction data
  • Purchase history: in-app purchase records processed through Google Play

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Enable location-based features and nearby discovery
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Send push notifications (with your permission)
  • Ensure safety and prevent misuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and analytics
  • Google Play for payment processing
  • Other users (only your public profile information and shared content)

4. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption. We use Firebase's security infrastructure to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information through the app settings
  • Opt out of location services at any time
  • Request account deletion by contacting us

6. Children's Safety

Gravity is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.
