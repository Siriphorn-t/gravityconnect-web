Child Safety Standards

Gravity — com.gravity.connect | Published by Gravity Connect

Gravity is committed to the safety and protection of all children. We have zero tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. Any content, behavior, or activity that exploits or endangers children is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate action.

1. Prohibition of CSAE

Gravity explicitly prohibits all forms of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including but not limited to:

  • Sharing, distributing, or soliciting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Grooming or any attempt to build a relationship with a minor for the purpose of sexual exploitation
  • Sextortion or any form of coercion involving minors
  • Any content that sexualizes minors in any way
  • Trafficking or facilitating the exploitation of children
  • Any communication with minors intended for sexual purposes

2. User Reporting Mechanism

Gravity provides an in-app reporting mechanism that allows users to report any content or behavior that violates these standards. Users can:

  • Report posts, comments, and user profiles directly within the app
  • Select specific violation categories including child safety concerns
  • Reports are reviewed by our moderation team promptly

3. Enforcement Actions

When violations of these standards are identified, Gravity will take immediate action, which may include:

  • Immediate removal of offending content
  • Permanent suspension of the offending account
  • Reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities
  • Cooperation with law enforcement investigations
  • Preservation of evidence as required by applicable laws

4. CSAM Detection and Response

Gravity maintains processes to detect and respond to CSAM:

  • All reported content is reviewed for potential CSAM
  • Confirmed CSAM is immediately removed and reported to NCMEC via CyberTipline
  • Accounts associated with CSAM are permanently banned

5. Compliance with Laws

Gravity complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including:

  • Thailand's Child Protection Act B.E. 2546 (2003)
  • Thailand's Computer Crime Act B.E. 2560 (2017)
  • United States PROTECT Act
  • 18 U.S.C. § 2258A (mandatory reporting requirements)
  • All applicable international child protection laws in jurisdictions where Gravity operates

6. Age Requirements

Gravity is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will take steps to remove their account and associated data.

For child safety concerns, reports, or inquiries, please contact our dedicated child safety team:

We aim to respond to all child safety reports within 24 hours.

8. Updates to These Standards

These Child Safety Standards may be updated periodically to reflect changes in laws, regulations, and best practices. The most current version will always be available at this URL.
